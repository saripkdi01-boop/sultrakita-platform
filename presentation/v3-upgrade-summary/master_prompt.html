01

Audit

Pahami repository, deployment, runtime, data, security, dan baseline test sebelum refactor.

02

P0 Foundation

Stabilkan authorization, ownership, verification, OTP, upload safety, persistence, dan parity runtime.

03

Product Upgrade

Bangun design system, discovery, listing flow, seller trust, social, messaging, dan notifications.

04

Release Readiness

Ukur performance, accessibility, SEO, PWA, observability, testing, deployment, dan rollback.
