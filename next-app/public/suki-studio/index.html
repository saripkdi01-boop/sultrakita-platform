05 /

Before you ask.

How do we communicate across timezones?

Async-first: written updates with preview links you can open anytime, plus scheduled video calls at hours that suit you. Our AI-assisted workflow means progress continues around the clock.

Who actually builds my website?

A senior human leads every project. AI agents accelerate research, code generation and QA — then the human reviews, refines and signs off. You get agency quality at freelancer speed.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 50% at launch. International transfers via Wise or bank transfer. Everything is agreed in writing before we begin.

What if I don't like the design?

The blueprint phase exists for this. You approve the direction before we build, and each tier includes structured revision rounds. We don't disappear after payment — that's the whole point.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes — care plans from $29/month: updates, backups, monitoring and small content changes. Optional, cancel anytime.
