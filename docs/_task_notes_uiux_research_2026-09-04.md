# Catatan Riset UI/UX SUKI Suits

## Airbnb

Airbnb menempatkan pencarian sebagai fungsi utama dan menggunakan kartu listing yang sangat visual. Kartu memprioritaskan gambar besar dengan kontrol simpan berbentuk heart, label status seperti “Guest favorite”, informasi lokasi/tipe, harga, serta rating. Pola carousel dan counter/dots perlu diterapkan pada kartu properti SUKI Suits; radius besar dan whitespace membantu persepsi premium.

Sumber: https://www.airbnb.com

## Zillow

Zillow memperlihatkan hierarki kategori Buy/Rent/Sell/Mortgage/Agent, pencarian alamat sebagai CTA utama, dan navigasi utilitas yang mudah diakses untuk Search, Updates, Favorites, Plan, dan Inbox. Pola split-view list + map dan search/filter yang tetap terlihat relevan untuk katalog properti. Badge status listing dan filter yang eksplisit juga perlu diserap.

Sumber: https://www.zillow.com

## Arah sintesis awal

SUKI Suits sebaiknya memadukan kartu visual premium dengan kontrol simpan, pencarian sticky, filter kontekstual, dan status listing yang ringkas. Elemen map bisa ditambahkan sebagai mode tampilan atau placeholder interaktif, bukan mengubah sidebar menjadi halaman listing penuh.

## Rumah123

Rumah123 uses clear Indonesian property modes (Dijual, Disewa, Properti Baru, Aset Bank, KPR) and exposes utility shortcuts such as Carikan Properti, Pasang Iklan, Cari Agen, Kalkulator KPR, and Take Over. Listing metadata favors concise numeric attributes such as bedroom/bathroom/parking and land/building area. Pricing is written in locally familiar forms such as **Rp 750 Juta**, **Rp 2 Jutaan/bulan**, and **Rp 30 Juta/tahun**. The homepage also makes mortgage simulation visible rather than burying it.

Sumber: https://www.rumah123.com

## 99.co Indonesia

99.co foregrounds the buy/rent choice, property type, and search, then surfaces “Properti Baru”, “Simulasi KPR”, “Kredit Multiguna”, and developer trust. It uses ranges such as **Rp451 Juta - 962 Juta** and labels the developer prominently. Trust language emphasizes verified sellers, convenience, and broad choice; financing is treated as a first-class journey.

Sumber: https://www.99.co/id (halaman menghadirkan captcha, sehingga temuan diambil dari konten tekstual yang berhasil diekstrak)

## SiKumbang

SiKumbang communicates institutional trust through government/association identity, developer login, supply-demand graphics, supply dashboard, and lookup modes for Wilayah Lokasi, Data Lokasi, ID Lokasi, and ID Rumah. For SUKI Suits this suggests a visible verification strip on property cards with a **SiKumbang ID**, developer/association identity, and remaining-unit/supply information.

Sumber: https://sikumbang.tapera.go.id

## Compass

Compass uses an image-led hero with a calm premium composition, serif-like editorial headline, and a focused omnibox that accepts properties or agents. Navigation separates Buy/Rent/Sell, Compass Exclusives, New Development, and Agents, while commercial and luxury categories are treated as explicit discovery paths. Listing cards use a large image sequence, prominent status label such as Coming Soon, price, and concise property stats.

Sumber: https://www.compass.com
